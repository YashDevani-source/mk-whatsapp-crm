## Lessons worth keeping
- Chrome's `--window-size` has a minimum width of about 500 px, so a "390 px" shot is not a phone. Use the skill's `shoot.mjs --w 390` (real device emulation) for phone widths.
- The taste skill is written for marketing websites; for app screens use its system rules (one card style, hover lift and press, one entrance, no violet, no fake controls) and skip its hero, photograph and finish-gate rules.
